CLAIM: Controlled demolition of the Twin Towers / WTC 7

TL;DR

NIST found 'no corroborating evidence' that the Twin Towers were brought down by pre-planted explosives — instead, photos and videos show the collapses initiated at the fire and impact floors and progressed downward.

Popular claim that explosives or thermite brought down WTC 1, 2 and 7. Counter-evidence: NIST NCSTAR 1 & 1A document a fire-driven progressive collapse mechanism with no blast evidence; NIST's FAQ quantitatively rebuts the thermite scenario. No primary source supports demolition.

Evidence status: Actively contradicted by primary sources.

AI review of the NIST NCSTAR 1 publication page plus the actual full report reached via its DOI link (nvlpubs.nist.gov, ~104,000 words extracted), read in key sections: - The document: "Federal Building and Fire Safety Investigation of the World Trade Center Disaster: Final Report of the National Construction Safety Team on the Collapses of the World Trade Center Towers (NIST NCSTAR 1)," published December 1, 2005, under the National Construction Safety Team Act, with 42 companion reports. - Mechanism: the abstract states the report describes "how the aircraft impacts and subsequent fires led to the collapse of the towers after terrorists flew jet fuel laden commercial airliners into the buildings." NIST based its conclusions on computer simulations, nearly 1,200 first-person interviews of occupants and responders, and copious photos and video. - Demolition hypothesis addressed — verbatim: "NIST found no corroborating evidence for alternative hypotheses suggesting that the WTC towers were brought down by controlled demolition using explosives planted prior to September 11, 2001. NIST also did not find any evidence that missiles were fired at or hit the towers. Instead, photographs and videos from several angles clearly show that the collapse initiated at the fire and impact floors and that the collapse progressed from the initiating floors downward, until the dust clouds obscured the view." - Output: 30 recommendations covering structural integrity, fire endurance, evacuation, and emergency response. - Summary check: the core is verified — NCSTAR 1 rejects demolition and establishes a fire-driven collapse.
