FBI 1992: Lanning's 'Investigator's Guide to Ritual Child Abuse'

TL;DR

This source is only page 20 of Lanning's 1992 'Investigator's Guide' — it shows Lanning pushing back on over-broad 'ritual abuse' labels (preferring 'bizarre' to 'ritual,' and 'multidimensional child sex rings'), noting alleged experts on such cases had never actually handled one before ('the answer was always the same — none!').

Supervisory Special Agent Kenneth V. Lanning (Behavioral Science Unit): no evidence of large-scale organized satanic cults abusing children in the US; crimes with bizarre/ritualistic elements do occur and must be distinguished from organized-cult claims; warned against suggestive interviewing. Frequently misquoted as a 'seven-year FBI study' — it is an investigator's guide, and Lanning did not declare all such claims a hoax.

Evidence status: Confirmed by primary sources — court records, government reports, declassified documents.
