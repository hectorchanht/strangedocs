J.P. Morgan: The Documented History

TL;DR

JPMorganChase's own history says J.P. Morgan died in 1913 and the Federal Reserve was established in 1913 'shortly after Morgan's death' — so he did not create the Fed.

John Pierpont Morgan (1837–1913) financed the railroads, steel and utilities that industrialized the US; formed U.S. Steel (1901). Died March 31, 1913 — months BEFORE the Federal Reserve Act (Dec 23, 1913). He did not create the Fed.
