Ghislaine Maxwell Trial & Conviction

TL;DR

A federal jury convicted Ghislaine Maxwell on Dec 29, 2021 of sex trafficking a minor and related conspiracies; on June 28, 2022 she was sentenced to 20 years in prison plus a $750,000 fine for a decade-long scheme (1994-2004) grooming girls as young as 14 for Epstein.

S.D.N.Y. trial Nov 29–Dec 29, 2021: four accuser witnesses testified under oath to grooming and sexual abuse as minors with Maxwell's assistance; exhibits included flight logs, the address book, photographs and financial records. Guilty on 5 of 6 counts; sentenced June 2022 to 20 years; affirmed by the Second Circuit (2024); Supreme Court declined review (Oct 2025). Note: trafficking of victims TO third parties was not proven at trial and remains alleged, not established.
