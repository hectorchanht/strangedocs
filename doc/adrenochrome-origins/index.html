Adrenochrome: From Fiction to QAnon

TL;DR

The claim that elites harvest children's blood for adrenochrome traces back to Hunter S. Thompson's 1971 fiction 'Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas,' not to any real evidence — and the chemical itself is readily synthesized in labs, no human sacrifice required.

Origin chain of the elite baby-eating/adrenochrome claim: Hunter S. Thompson's 1971 FICTION 'Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas' → Gilliam's 1998 film → 4chan /x/ (2013) and /pol/ (2014) → Pizzagate (2016) → QAnon (2017+). Scientific reality: adrenochrome is a real chemical (oxidized adrenaline) — unstable, no fountain-of-youth properties, synthesized industrially. NO primary source — no court filing, FBI file, testimony or DOJ release — connects Epstein or any public figure to adrenochrome harvesting or ritual sacrifice.

Evidence status: Confirmed by primary sources — court records, government reports, declassified documents.
