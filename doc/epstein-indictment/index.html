2019 SDNY Indictment (19 Cr. 490)

TL;DR

The unsealed 2019 SDNY indictment charged Epstein with sex trafficking of minors and conspiracy, alleging he exploited dozens of girls as young as 14 at his Manhattan and Palm Beach homes from 2002 to 2005; the case was dismissed after his death.

United States v. Jeffrey Epstein: conspiracy to commit sex trafficking of minors (§371) and sex trafficking of minors (§1591), alleging conduct 2002–2005 in New York and Palm Beach — girls as young as 14 enticed to 'massages' escalating to sex acts for cash, victims recruited to recruit others. Epstein was found dead August 10, 2019 (suicide, per the NYC medical examiner); the criminal case abated. Guilt was never adjudicated.
