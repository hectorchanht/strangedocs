Iran-Contra: Tower Commission & Walsh Report

TL;DR

The Tower Commission (Feb 26, 1987) found the NSC ran covert arms sales to Iran — whose excess profits were very likely diverted to the Contras — while President Reagan's hands-off management style meant he never forced the operation through real review or accountability.

The Reagan administration secretly sold arms to Iran and diverted proceeds to Nicaragua's Contras despite the Boland Amendment ban. Tower Commission (Feb 1987): Reagan's 'lax managerial style.' Walsh final report (Aug 1993): 14 officials charged, 11 convictions/guilty pleas; arms sales 'contravened U.S. Government policy'; systematic document destruction documented. George H.W. Bush's Dec 24, 1992 pardons ended the probe.

Evidence status: Confirmed by primary sources — court records, government reports, declassified documents.
