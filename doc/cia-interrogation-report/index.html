Senate Report: CIA Detention & Interrogation (2014)

TL;DR

The Senate Intelligence Committee's five-year review found the CIA's 'enhanced interrogation' program was ineffective, built on false claims to policymakers, deeply mismanaged — and far more brutal than the CIA admitted, with sleep deprivation up to 180 hours and a detainee dying of suspected hypothermia.

Five-year SSCI study of 6.3M+ pages of CIA records; 500+ page declassified executive summary. Findings: 'enhanced interrogation techniques' were not effective; the CIA gave extensively inaccurate information about the program to policymakers and the public; management was deeply flawed; the program was far more brutal than represented (waterboarding, systematic beatings, sleep deprivation; 119+ detainees in secret 'black sites').

Evidence status: Confirmed by primary sources — court records, government reports, declassified documents.
