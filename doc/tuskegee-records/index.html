Tuskegee Syphilis Experiment: The Records

TL;DR

The CDC's official account: the U.S. Public Health Service's Untreated Syphilis Study at Tuskegee (1932-1972) enrolled 600 Black men — 399 with syphilis, 201 without — never collected informed consent, withheld available treatment, and ended in 1972; Clinton issued a formal presidential apology in 1997.

USPHS study in Macon County, Alabama (1932–1972): 399 Black men with syphilis observed without treatment; penicillin withheld after 1947; exposed by AP in 1972 (Peter Buxtun leak); terminated the same year. Class action Pollard v. United States settled 1974 (~$10M + lifetime medical benefits). President Clinton's formal apology, May 16, 1997, White House ceremony.

Evidence status: Confirmed by primary sources — court records, government reports, declassified documents.
